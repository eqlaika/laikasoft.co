# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

EverQuest multiboxers who use Stonemite on Windows and its companion Stream Deck plugin. They need an official place to understand the products, download Stonemite, report bugs, request features, and contact the developer when they are stuck.

## Product Purpose

Laikasoft is the official home and support site for small EverQuest utilities. It succeeds when a Stonemite user can quickly identify the right product, get the software, and choose the right support path without needing to search elsewhere.

## Positioning

Stonemite keeps an EverQuest box crew visible and controllable without repeatedly navigating Windows. Its Stream Deck companion carries the same live character identity and narrowly bounded controls onto Stream Deck hardware or Stream Deck Mobile.

## Operating Context

- Stonemite runs on Windows for EverQuest multiboxing.
- The companion Stream Deck plugin is being prepared for publication and will be presented as **Stonemite for Stream Deck**, replacing the internal **Ikkinz** name in public-facing copy.
- Support is provided by email and through GitHub issues.
- The website is an Astro static site currently deployed with GitHub Pages at `https://laikasoft.co`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site currently presents Stonemite and links to its GitHub repository and latest release.
- The support page covers both Stonemite and Stonemite for Stream Deck.
- Email is the direct support path; GitHub issues are the public path for reproducible bugs and feature requests.
- The site does not currently provide accounts, forms, live chat, a knowledge base, or service-level response commitments.

## Brand Commitments

- Company name: **Laikasoft**.
- Product names: **Stonemite** and **Stonemite for Stream Deck** in public-facing website copy.
- Voice is concise, direct, technical, and sentence case.
- Existing assets in `public/` and the incumbent dark, compact website remain the visual authority.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing website copy and routes in `src/pages/`.
- Stonemite product artwork in `public/stonemite.png` and `public/stonemite-small.png`.
- The Stonemite source, releases, and issue tracker at `https://github.com/eqlaika/stonemite`.
- Contact address: `laika@laikasoft.co`.
- There are no testimonials, support-response guarantees, or customer claims to present.

## Product Principles

1. Get users to the right support path quickly.
2. Keep product naming and ownership unambiguous.
3. Prefer direct, honest technical language over marketing claims.
4. Link to authoritative downloads, source, and issue tracking rather than duplicating them.
5. Do not promise support channels or response times that do not exist.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, descriptive link labels, sufficient contrast, and layouts that remain readable on narrow mobile screens. Meaning must not depend on color alone.
